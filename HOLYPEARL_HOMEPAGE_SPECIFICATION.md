# HolyPearl Homepage Specification

Purpose: reposition HolyPearl.co.il from a product-led WooCommerce store into a trusted Judaica guidance and discovery website, while keeping inquiry and product interest commercially useful.

Primary conversion goal: WhatsApp inquiry.

Secondary conversion goals: browse Judaica categories, read guides, submit contact form.

## 1. Final Section Order

1. Hero Section
2. Trust Bar
3. Introduction / Positioning Section
4. Judaica Categories Section
5. Featured Guidance Section
6. Personal Help / WhatsApp Section
7. About Preview Section
8. Reviews / Social Proof Section
9. Newsletter / Lead Magnet Section
10. Final CTA Section

## 14. Global Implementation Notes

- Use full-width rows only for hero, trust bar, WhatsApp CTA, reviews, and final CTA.
- Use standard-width content rows for educational and category sections.
- Save reusable CTA rows as Beaver Builder global rows.
- Use Beaver Builder's responsive settings to reduce padding on mobile.
- Avoid WooCommerce product grids on the homepage.
- Replace Shop Now language with Explore, Learn, Ask, and Get Guidance.
- Keep WhatsApp visible in the header and at least twice in the homepage body.
